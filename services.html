Time Capsule

Save the moments that matter. Time Capsule gives students a special space to preserve meaningful memories throughout their academic journey. From important achievements and special events to unforgettable moments with friends, students can capture the experiences they will want to remember in the future. Because your student journey is not only about what you achieve, but also about the moments you create along the way.

Supplies

Everything you need, right when you need it. Supplies was created to make student life more practical and organized. It helps students keep track of the materials and resources they need for their academic activities, so they can prepare ahead of time and avoid forgetting something important. Stay prepared. Stay organized. Be ready to learn.

Expert Tips

Expert Tips and Supplies offer quick wellness guides and effective study techniques that promote healthier habits and better learning outcomes. Guidance that goes beyond the classroom. Expert Tips provides students with useful advice and recommendations to help them improve their academic habits, organization, productivity, and overall well-being. Each tip is designed to offer practical support that students can apply to their everyday lives. Small changes can create better habits, and better habits can lead to bigger goals.

SuPlanner

Suplanner was born from the real needs of students. Inspired by everyday life in the Supérate program, it was created to simplify organization and support students in their journey.
